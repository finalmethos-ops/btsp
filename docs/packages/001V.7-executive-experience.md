# 001V.7 — Executive Reporting Experience

## Outcome

001V.7 gives executive users a live spend view directly on the standard
Purchasing Intelligence command center.

## Command-center intelligence

- Month-to-date landed purchase-order spend by entity
- Year-to-date landed purchase-order spend by entity
- Top ten month-to-date products by ordered units, with landed spend shown
- Separate totals and rankings by currency
- Automatic refresh every minute and whenever the page becomes visible
- Executive-only rendering in the standard portal
- Direct drill-through from the command center to detailed analytics
- One-click download of the complete executive Excel report pack

## Executive exports

- All immediate and scheduled analytics exports use Excel workbooks.
- Every workbook opens on a branded dashboard and retains complete detail data.
- The complete executive report pack combines spend, product, vendor, workflow,
  and inventory intelligence in one seven-tab workbook.

No schema migration is required. The dashboard is calculated from durable
purchase-order lines and the official store/entity directory.
